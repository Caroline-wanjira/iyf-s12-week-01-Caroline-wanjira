Three Habits for a Steadier Budget

By Caroline Wanjira on 2 October 2026

A budget rarely fails because of one big mistake. It drifts off course through small, unnoticed choices. These three habits keep it steady.

1. Pay yourself first

Move a fixed amount into savings the day your income arrives. What is left is what you spend.

2. Review once a week

Ten minutes a week is enough to spot a forgotten subscription or a category that is creeping up.

3. Plan for irregular costs

Divide yearly costs by twelve and set that amount aside each month.
